Teaching tool — not for clinical use. All cases are fictional and written for instruction. They are simplified to foreground a teaching point; real presentations are rarely this clean, and diagnosis in practice requires a full assessment, collateral information, medical and substance rule-outs, and cultural context. Always verify criteria against the current DSM-5-TR. Diagnosis is a licensed clinical activity performed within scope of practice and supervision.

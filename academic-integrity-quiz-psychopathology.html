Course policy — read before you begin

Generative AI is not permitted

Using AI of any kind (e.g., ChatGPT, Grammarly, QuillBot) to generate, revise, or complete academic work is considered plagiarism. Any assignment with AI-generated content receives a zero and may result in a zero for the course. If you are unsure whether a tool or process counts as AI use, ask the instructor before submitting — asking in advance is encouraged.

Do your own work

Write in your own voice and cite the textbook and DSM-5-TR in APA 7th. Ground claims in specific concepts and criteria in your own words, and use concrete details a generic answer would miss — including from your own de-identified practicum experience. Discussions need an initial post plus substantive replies to two classmates by name.

Originality checks

Some papers may be submitted to Turnitin to verify originality. You may opt out within two weeks of the start of the term, and the instructor will use alternative methods.

Deadlines & late work

Assignments are due Mondays at 11:59 p.m. CST; this quiz is due Sunday, Aug 23, and quizzes are not reopened once the due date passes. Late work is accepted only for documented extenuating circumstances — contact the instructor promptly, and before the deadline when foreseeable.

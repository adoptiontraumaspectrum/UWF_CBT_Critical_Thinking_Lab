Weigh the Tradeoffs

In the room, there's rarely a single right move — there are competing goods and real costs. This exercise trains the judgment the manual can't script.

For each scenario, choose the response you find most defensible and the one you find least defensible, and jot your reasoning. Then reveal an analysis of every option's tradeoffs. Several options are genuinely defensible; the goal isn't to guess a key — it's to reason about why.

The habit to keep

Strong clinical reasoning names what each option prioritizes and what it costs — safety, alliance, fidelity, momentum — before committing. When you disagreed with the analysis, the useful question isn't "was I wrong?" but "which value was I weighting, and is that the right call for this client, right now?"

This is a reasoning exercise, not a graded quiz. Nothing is submitted or stored.

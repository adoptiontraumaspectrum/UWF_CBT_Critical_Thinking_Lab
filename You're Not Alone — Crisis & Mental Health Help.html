Crisis & mental health resources

You matter, and help is here.

If you're feeling suicidal, overwhelmed, or just need someone to talk to, you don't have to carry it alone. You don't need the right words, and you don't have to be in crisis to reach out. Below are people who are ready to help — right now, and for the longer road too.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger

If there's a risk to life happening right now, this is what 911 is for. It's okay to call.

Call 911
Free · Confidential · 24/7

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Talk or text with a trained counselor any time, about anything — suicidal thoughts, a mental health or substance use crisis, or any kind of emotional distress. You can also reach out because you're worried about someone else.

Call 988 Text 988 Chat online

Veterans & service members: call 988, then press 1. En español: llama y presiona 2, o envía AYUDA al 988. Deaf/Hard of hearing: 988 offers ASL video support.

Escambia County Local help in Pensacola

Services based right in Escambia County and the surrounding Northwest Florida area, staffed by people who know the community.

Lakeview Center — Mobile Response Team

24/7 · comes to you

A mental health crisis team for Escambia and Northwest Florida that meets people where they are — at home, school, or in the community. Call for phone support or an in-person response during a crisis. Lakeview also operates the county's Central Receiving Facility (adult Baker Act screening for Escambia & Santa Rosa) and a Crisis Stabilization Unit on its downtown Pensacola campus.

☎1-866-517-7766 ☎850-281-5334

211 Northwest Florida

24/7 · local

United Way of West Florida's local team answers both 2-1-1 and 988 calls for the Pensacola area, offering crisis counseling and referrals to nearby help. Serves Escambia, Santa Rosa, and neighboring counties.

☎Dial 211 ✉Text your ZIP to 898211 211nwfl.org

UWF Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS)

UWF students

Free, confidential counseling, triage, and psychiatric care for currently enrolled University of West Florida students. Call to set up a triage appointment (Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm CT); walk-in crisis evaluations are offered 8–10am and 1–4pm. The same number reaches an after-hours phone counselor — just call and follow the prompts. On campus in Building 960, Suite 200A (11000 University Parkway). Students can also use TalkCampus, a free anonymous peer-support app (sign up with a UWF email). In immediate danger on campus, call UWF Police or 911.

☎850-474-2420 ☎Campus Police 850-474-2415 UWF CAPS

This sheet is for information and support; it isn't a substitute for professional or emergency care. Services and numbers can change — if something doesn't connect, dial 988 or 211 and they can point you the right way. Compiled August 2026.

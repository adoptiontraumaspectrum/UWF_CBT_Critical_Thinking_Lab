The distinction that matters

Flexibility and drift can look alike from the outside — both depart from the plan. What separates them is whether the departure is principled and protects the core (attending to safety or emotion, then returning to structure) or simply lets the structure dissolve (skipped homework review, agenda-free supportive talk). Ask of any deviation: what core component am I protecting, and will I return to the arc?

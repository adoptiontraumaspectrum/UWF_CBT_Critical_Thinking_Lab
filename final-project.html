Case Conceptualization Paper

Your capstone: a polished, individualized case conceptualization and treatment plan that integrates the term's learning into a coherent clinical picture.

▸ Due Monday, November 30, 2026 · 11:59 p.m. CST · submit via the LMS drop box

The assignment

Select one client case (your assigned case, or one approved by the instructor) and write a complete CBT case conceptualization and treatment plan. The paper should read as a working clinical document — the kind you would bring to supervision — showing not just what you would do but why, grounded in the cognitive model and the manualized approach practiced across the course. Your earlier writing exercises, the Case Conceptualization Lab, and the three critical-thinking exercises are all scaffolding for this final synthesis.

Format: approximately 8–12 double-spaced pages, APA 7th edition, with a signed integrity statement. Use headings that map to the components below.

Required components

1 · Presenting problem & referral context

Who the client is, what brings them in, the referral pathway, and the presenting concerns in their own words and yours.

2 · Assessment & measures

DSM-5 diagnostic reasoning for GAD (or the relevant presentation), plus selection, scoring, and interpretation of measures (e.g., GAD-7 and PSWQ), distinguishing state severity from trait worry.

3 · Case formulation

A cognitive formulation: situations, automatic thoughts, emotions, physical sensations, behaviours (including safety behaviours), core beliefs, and the maintaining factors that keep the problem going — with the client's strengths and protective factors named.

4 · Treatment plan

A session-by-session plan grounded in the manualized approach: goals, sequenced interventions (worry time, exposure, restructuring, core-belief work), measurable targets, and how you balance fidelity with principled flexibility.

5 · Cultural responsiveness & ethics

How you adapt the manual responsively to this client's context and identity, and the ethical tensions you anticipate in standardized treatment.

6 · Relapse prevention & termination

An early-warning-signs and coping plan, the lapse-vs-relapse distinction, and a planned, collaborative approach to termination.

Oral defense

As described in the syllabus, the reasoning in your paper is verified through a brief live oral defense: you'll walk through your formulation and treatment decisions and respond to questions. The defense confirms the clinical reasoning is demonstrably your own.

Academic integrity This paper must be your own clinical reasoning.
